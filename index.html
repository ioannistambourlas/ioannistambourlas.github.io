CourtSense

A badminton coach that works from a phone video. It finds the players with YOLOv8, tracks their pose with MediaPipe, maps positions onto the court, and turns footwork and coverage stats into written feedback. Shuttle tracking is next.

Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, YOLOv8, MediaPipe, pytest

About me

I grew up in Nicosia, finished school with 19.2 out of 20 while travelling to tournaments, and moved to Stockholm in 2025 to study at KTH.

The software I enjoy most is the kind that has to be right: payments that can’t charge someone twice, data that has to stay private, systems that keep working when a few hundred people open them at once. Futsalaki taught me a lot about that, and my courses in algorithms, digital design and embedded systems gave me the fundamentals to reason about it properly.

For summer 2027 I’d like to join a team that ships to real users, learn how experienced engineers review and test their work, and be given something real to own. I pick things up quickly, I take feedback well, and I’m used to demanding training schedules.
