Futsalaki

The player app: find an opponent at your level, book a pitch, pay your share and keep score in a league.

  1. Join an open match from the home screen and pay
  2. Find a match — the seven-step booking flow
  3. Check the leaderboard
  4. Open My team and report a score — both captains must agree
Interactive demo with sample data, recreated from the live app for this portfolio. Nothing is charged or sent anywhere. The real app is on the App Store and Google Play.
